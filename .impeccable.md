## Design Context

### Users
Changed 27.7.2026, on the founder's call. Previously "AI agent builders".

A regular Israeli shopper who already talks to Claude or ChatGPT and is tired of
guessing which supermarket is cheaper. Not a developer. They do not know what MCP
is and should not have to. Developers are a real secondary audience but they can
find what they need behind one disclosure; shoppers cannot decode jargon.

### Brand Personality
Plain-spoken and straight. Talks about groceries and money, not protocols. Warm
without being cute, and never overstates what it knows: it says what is missing,
what is estimated, and when a price was last seen.

### Aesthetic Direction
"Sticker ledger", adopted 4.8.2026, replacing the operational-ledger direction.
Neo-brutalism translated for a grocery ledger: the page is a sheet of cream
price-tag paper, and everything important arrives as a sticker slapped on it.

- Hebrew RTL. Cream paper (never white), warm near-black ink. Ink carries ALL
  text, ALL borders (2.5-3.5px) and ALL shadows
- **The sticker is the card.** Bordered, hard-shadowed (offset down-LEFT because
  RTL: `-5px 5px 0 0 ink`, tokenised as `--shadow-sticker*`), often rotated a
  degree or two. Interactive stickers press: hover slides them toward their
  shadow (`.pressable`). Radius is tight: 8px cards, pill chips
- Colour: loud grape is the brand colour (graphics, bands, the logo sticker);
  lime is savings/wins/the CTA and always takes ink text; tangerine is
  decoration only. Deltas and missing lines use `--color-over` (AA-safe on
  cream), never raw tangerine. Two full-width colour fields per page max: the
  grape ledger band and the lime access band
- **Numbers still carry the page.** The sticker ledger does not change what the
  product is: every figure goes through `.figure` (mono, tabular), every Latin
  or currency run through `.ltr`
- Texture comes from the dot grid, the marquee ticker between hero and ledger,
  and hand-drawn doodles (Sparkle, PixelPlus, Squiggle, HandArrow in
  `components/shared/doodles.tsx`). Decoration decorates; it never sits under
  body copy
- Photography stays in one bordered strip with no text over it; un-retouched
  chat screenshots remain welcome as evidence, never fake UI mockups
- Fonts: Secular One display, Heebo body, Geist Mono numbers/code, Archivo
  Black for the Latin wordmark only
- No em/en dashes in copy (Hebrew maqaf is fine)

### Design Principles
1. Five-second comprehension: רשימה נכנסת → תוכנית קנייה יוצאת. The hero shows
   that transformation with real data rather than describing it
2. **No published number without a measurement behind it.** Every figure lives in
   `apps/web/src/content/he.ts` with the date it was measured and a footnote
   saying what it is not. Comparisons must be like-for-like, never raw totals
   over different line counts
3. Show the gaps. The missing line, the coverage ratio and the freshness stamp are
   features, not embarrassments: they are the reason to trust the rest
4. **Plain Hebrew above the fold and everywhere else.** If a word only means
   something to someone who builds software (שרת, SDK, endpoint, לקוח in the API
   sense, tool names), it belongs inside the developer disclosure in the connect
   section. There is an automated check for this in the session notes: walk the
   text nodes of `main`, skip anything inside `<details>`, and the jargon list
   must come back empty
5. One dominant CTA intent (התחילו עכשיו) leading to the inline access form. The
   label is a verb the reader performs; the cost and the wait are answered in
   microcopy directly beneath it, never crammed into the label
6. MCP, `mcp.json`, the tool list and self-hosting live behind a single
   disclosure. A shopper never opens it, a developer finds it in one click
7. **Never hide content behind JavaScript.** Reveals are CSS-only progressive
   enhancement (`data-enter` / `data-reveal` in `globals.css`); the copy is
   visible in the stylesheet, with or without JS, reduced motion or view timelines
8. Text meets WCAG AA at its own size. `--color-ink-faint` is the lightest tint
   allowed on body copy and is already at the floor
