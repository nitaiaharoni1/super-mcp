## Design Context

### Users
AI agent builders who need Israeli grocery shopping plans for agents — without decoding API schemas first.

### Brand Personality
Friendly-operational. Wolt Market clarity: airy Hebrew landing, one loud CTA, grocery visuals. Super MCP truth: asks before uncertain swaps.

### Aesthetic Direction
"Operational ledger", adopted 27.7.2026, replacing the earlier photo-led direction.

- Hebrew RTL; deep cyan accent, neutrals tinted toward hue 230, paper is never pure white
- **Numbers carry the page.** The recurring motif is the hairline rule and the
  tabular figure, not the rounded card, because the artifact this product
  produces is a price list. Every figure goes through `.figure` (mono, tabular)
  and every Latin or currency run through `.ltr`
- The measured price ledger is the peak and the only colour block on the page
- Photography is allowed in exactly one full-bleed strip with no text over it.
  A photo never occupies a slot where an argument belongs. Un-retouched chat
  screenshots are still welcome as evidence; never fake UI mockups
- Fonts: Secular One display, Heebo body, Geist Mono for numbers/code; radius 16/24/pill
- Max ~2 eyebrows per page; no em/en dashes in copy (Hebrew maqaf is fine)

### Design Principles
1. Five-second comprehension: רשימה נכנסת → תוכנית קנייה יוצאת. The hero shows
   that transformation with real data rather than describing it
2. **No published number without a measurement behind it.** Every figure lives in
   `apps/web/src/content/he.ts` with the date it was measured and a footnote
   saying what it is not. Comparisons must be like-for-like, never raw totals
   over different line counts
3. Show the gaps. The missing line, the coverage ratio and the freshness stamp are
   features, not embarrassments: they are the reason to trust the rest
4. One dominant CTA intent (בקשו מפתח) leading to the inline access form (no mailto)
5. The audience is an agent builder. The MCP URL, the `mcp.json` and the tool list
   are open on the page, not behind a disclosure
6. **Never hide content behind JavaScript.** Reveals are CSS-only progressive
   enhancement (`data-enter` / `data-reveal` in `globals.css`); the copy is
   visible in the stylesheet, with or without JS, reduced motion or view timelines
7. Text meets WCAG AA at its own size. `--color-ink-faint` is the lightest tint
   allowed on body copy and is already at the floor
